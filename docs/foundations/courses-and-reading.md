# Courses, reading, and community

Free, high-quality material for learning AI safety beyond this site. Everything here was checked to be available
in October 2026.

## Courses

| Resource | What it is | Best for | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [AI Safety, Ethics, and Society](https://www.aisafetybook.com/) | Free textbook and lectures by Dan Hendrycks (Center for AI Safety) | The broadest single introduction | 🟢 |
| [BlueDot Impact: AI Alignment](https://bluedot.org/courses/alignment) | Free structured course with readings and discussion groups | A guided introduction with peers | 🟢 |
| [BlueDot Impact: Frontier AI Governance](https://bluedot.org/courses/governance) | Free governance and policy course | The policy track | 🟢 |
| [ARENA](https://www.arena.education/) ([materials](https://github.com/ARENA-education/ARENA_materials)) | Coding curriculum: transformers, interpretability, RL, evals | The technical track | 🟡 |
| [Neel Nanda's mech interp quickstart](https://www.neelnanda.io/mechanistic-interpretability/quickstart) | Guide to getting started in mechanistic interpretability | Interpretability | 🟡 |
| [ControlArena: Introduction to Control](https://control-arena.aisi.org.uk/intro-to-control.html) | Practical introduction to AI control | AI control | 🔴 |
| [Inspect documentation](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/) | Full guide to the evaluation framework used in the tutorials | Building evaluations | 🟡 |

AISafety.com keeps up-to-date lists of [self-study material](https://www.aisafety.com/courses) and
[training programmes](https://aisafety.training/).

## Foundational papers, by topic

**Overviews**

- [Concrete Problems in AI Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565) (Amodei et al., 2016): the classic early research agenda.
- [Unsolved Problems in ML Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.13916) (Hendrycks et al., 2021)
- [An Overview of Catastrophic AI Risks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12001) (Hendrycks et al., 2023)
- [International AI Safety Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17805) (Bengio et al., 2025)
- [Foundational Challenges in Assuring Alignment and Safety of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09932) (Anwar et al., 2024)

**Alignment theory**

- [Risks from Learned Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01820) (Hubinger et al., 2019)
- [The Alignment Problem from a Deep Learning Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.00626) (Ngo et al., 2022)
- [Goal Misgeneralization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01790) (Shah et al., 2022)

**Training for safety**

- [Deep RL from Human Preferences](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03741) (Christiano et al., 2017)
- [InstructGPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155) (Ouyang et al., 2022)
- [Constitutional AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) (Bai et al., 2022)
- [Open Problems and Fundamental Limitations of RLHF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15217) (Casper et al., 2023)
- [Weak-to-Strong Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09390) (Burns et al., 2023)

**Interpretability**

- [Zoom In: An Introduction to Circuits](https://distill.pub/2020/circuits/zoom-in/) (Olah et al., 2020)
- [Toy Models of Superposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.10652) (Elhage et al., 2022)
- [Scaling Monosemanticity](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2024/scaling-monosemanticity/) (Templeton et al., 2024)
- [Mechanistic Interpretability for AI Safety: A Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14082) (Bereska & Gavves, 2024)

**Evaluation, deception, and control**: see the "Key papers" section of each [method guide](../methods/index.md).

## Communities and news

- [AI Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org/): technical alignment research and discussion.
- [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/): broader discussion, including many AI safety posts.
- [Transformer Circuits Thread](https://transformer-circuits.pub/): interpretability research from Anthropic.
- Research organisations with readable output: [Center for AI Safety](https://www.safe.ai/),
  [METR](https://metr.org/), [Apollo Research](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/),
  [Redwood Research](https://www.redwoodresearch.org/), [UK AI Security Institute](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/work),
  and the [Centre for the Governance of AI](https://www.governance.ai/).

## Careers

- [80,000 Hours: Preventing an AI-related catastrophe](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/artificial-intelligence/):
  an introduction to the problem and career paths, plus their [podcast](https://80000hours.org/podcast/).
- [MATS](https://www.matsprogram.org/): a research mentorship programme for aspiring alignment researchers.
- A small, finished project (see [Stage 4 of the roadmap](index.md#stage-4)) is one of the
  best things to show in an application.
